# MapVoice Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 17-June-2026

MapVoice is a prototype application that demonstrates pronunciation-aware normalization for Indian navigation-style instructions. This Privacy Policy explains what information MapVoice processes and how it is used.

## Information We Collect

MapVoice does not require users to create an account or sign in.

When you use the app, you may enter or select a navigation-style instruction, such as a road name or place name. This text is sent to the MapVoice backend so that the app can normalize the text and generate speech output.

Examples of processed text may include:

* road names
* place names
* navigation-style phrases
* manually entered sample instructions

MapVoice does not intentionally collect:

* your name
* your email address
* your phone number
* your contacts
* your photos
* your precise device location
* your payment information

## How We Use Information

The text you enter is used only to:

* normalize navigation-style instructions
* generate pronunciation-friendly text
* create raw and normalized speech audio
* display the comparison result in the app

## Third-Party Services

MapVoice may send the text you enter to third-party text-to-speech services, such as Sarvam AI, to generate audio output.

These third-party services process the submitted text for speech generation. Their handling of data is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

## Generated Audio

MapVoice generates audio files for raw and normalized speech playback. These files may be temporarily stored or cached on the backend server to allow playback in the app.

Generated audio is used only for demonstrating the speech comparison feature.

## Data Retention

MapVoice is a prototype. User-entered text and generated audio may be temporarily processed or cached for app functionality. We do not use this data to create user profiles.

## Data Sharing

MapVoice does not sell user data.

User-entered text may be shared with service providers only when necessary to provide the app’s text normalization and speech generation features.

## Children’s Privacy

MapVoice is not intended for children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect the data processed by the app. However, no internet-based service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
